# User-selected equipment icons

Original MapleStory artwork © NEXON, retrieved through [MapleStory.io](https://maplestory.io/).

These PNGs are the original responses, without cropping, recoloring, or AI generation. `sources.json` records the verified Korean item names, item IDs, provider region/version, URLs, retrieval times, dimensions, byte lengths, and SHA-256 hashes. The older original item IDs are used where modern/event variants share a name.

The selected illustrations apply to matching slots in all supported jobs. Shoulder illustrations also apply to custom slots named 어깨장식/견장; this does not create a new equipped slot. Other slots keep their existing illustrations.

These are representative images, not OCR identification of a player's equipment. Provider stats, levels, and equipment restrictions are not imported into the calculator. Assets are served by the app without runtime requests to the provider. No private screenshots are included.
